How SIKKA Works

Follow the journey from account creation to mining rewards

Register Account

Create your account with secure authentication and user verification

Wallet Auto-Created

A unique digital wallet address is automatically generated for you

Send SIKKA Coins

Transfer coins to other users through secure peer-to-peer transactions

Transaction Pool

Transactions enter the pending pool awaiting block inclusion

Mine a Block

Validate transactions by solving proof-of-work puzzles

Blockchain Updated

New block is cryptographically linked to the blockchain

Rewards Credited

Mining rewards are credited to your wallet automatically
